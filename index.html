How It Works

Learn how HeadlineHub helps you stay informed and prepared for UIL Current Events.

Step 1: News is collected throughout the day

HeadlineHub gathers recent stories from selected news sources throughout the day.

Step 2: Quizzes are created

Current stories are turned into UIL-style practice questions based on important people, places, and events.

Step 3: Practice and Review

Take quizzes, review your answers, and return to the original news source when you need more context.

About HeadlineHub

HeadlineHub was created to make preparing for UIL Current Events easier. It brings together recent news articles and turns important stories into practice questions so students can study in one place.
